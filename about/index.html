Ancient cities, mountain mornings and the people you meet along the way. Discover Sri Lanka at your own pace, with a local team by your side.

OUR STORY

A local team.
A personal way to travel.

Founded by Nilanka Lakmal, Ranweli Tours is a Sri Lankan team that helps travelers plan private trips around the island. The team works with local businesses and communities to arrange visits and experiences along each route.

Start with a published itinerary or tell the team what interests you. They can shape your travel days around temples, tea country, wildlife and the coast, and arrange a suitable vehicle for your group.
