Sri Lanka in 8 Days

Explore ancient cities, climb Sigiriya and make time for tea country, wildlife and the south coast.

Your itinerary

  1. Day 01

    See Anuradhapura and Mihintale, then stay in Sigiriya

  2. Day 02

    Explore Polonnaruwa and look for elephants on safari at Hurulu, Kaudulla or Minneriya

  3. Day 03

    Visit Sigiriya or Pidurangala, Dambulla and Matale en route to Kandy

  4. Day 04

    Discover Kandy’s Temple of the Tooth, viewpoints and botanical gardens

  5. Day 05

    Travel from Kandy through tea country and Nuwara Eliya

  6. Day 06

    Visit Ella’s railway landmarks and hike Little Adam’s Peak

  7. Day 07

    Leave Ella for southern temples and a wildlife outing

  8. Day 08

    Explore Galle’s Dutch Fort on the final day
