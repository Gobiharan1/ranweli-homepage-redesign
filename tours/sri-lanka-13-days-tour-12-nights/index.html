Sri Lanka in 13 Days

Go north to Jaffna before circling back through the Cultural Triangle, the hill country and southern coast.

Your itinerary

  1. Day 01

    Visit Anuradhapura and continue to Sigiriya

  2. Day 02

    Travel north to Jaffna for Tamil culture and heritage

  3. Day 03

    Visit beaches around Jaffna

  4. Day 04

    Visit Kandy’s gardens and craft stops before continuing to Sigiriya

  5. Day 05

    Explore Sigiriya, with an optional hot-air balloon outing

  6. Day 06

    Discover Buddhist sites and Matale’s spice gardens

  7. Day 07

    Visit botanical gardens, Pinnawala and a gem stop

  8. Day 08

    Climb Ambuluwawa Tower and visit Nuwara Eliya’s tea country

  9. Day 09

    Explore Little Adam’s Peak, Nine Arch Bridge and Ravana Falls in Ella

  10. Day 10

    Travel towards Mirissa via southern heritage sites and Yala

  11. Day 11

    Spend time around Mirissa and Galle, with whale watching when arranged

  12. Day 12

    Continue to Bentota, with the option of a Madu River outing

  13. Day 13

    Visit Colombo before departure
